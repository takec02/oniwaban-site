Privacy Policy

Yuhitsu — an email app for iPhone and Mac

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short

Yuhitsu does not carry your mail anywhere. Reading it, working out what it asks for, and building the address book all happen on your own device. We run no servers, so we have no way of receiving your mail in the first place.

There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is passed to a third party.

Where things are kept

Your mail Fetched from your own mail server straight to this device. It is stored on the device, inside the app's own area.
Your mail password Kept only in the device keychain. If you choose "Sign in" for Gmail or Microsoft 365, we never hold the password at all.
What the AI does It uses Apple's model, built into the device. On the Mac you may switch to an Ollama installation of your own. We never receive your mail. Note that if you pick one of Ollama's cloud models, Ollama sends the body of the message to its own cloud service for processing.
The address book Built on the device from the mail you receive. It does not leave the device.
Scanning a business card The text is read out of the photo by the device itself (Vision). Neither the photo nor the result leaves the device.
Device to device Over the same Wi-Fi, or over Bluetooth, directly between the two devices and through no server. The exchange is encrypted, and connecting requires a six-digit code shown on both screens.

What it connects to

Yuhitsu connects to these three things and nothing else.

  • The mail server you set up (IMAP and SMTP, over an encrypted connection)
  • Google's or Microsoft's sign-in page — only if you choose "Sign in". The token they issue is kept in the device keychain
  • Ollama — only if you choose it as the AI engine. By default that is your own machine (localhost). If you point it at another machine yourself, the body of your mail goes to that machine. If you pick one of Ollama's cloud models, Ollama sends the body to its own cloud service for processing

There is nothing else. There is no server of ours for anything to go to.

Subscriptions

Payment is handled by Apple. All we learn is whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your name, your email address, or your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the mail, the address book, the settings and the password kept on the device along with it. The mail on your mail server is yours, and is not touched.

Children

Yuhitsu collects no personal data, so nothing in it is handled differently by age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top is changed with it.
