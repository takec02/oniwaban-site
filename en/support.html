Common questions

Does my mail ever leave the device?
No. Reading it and judging it both happen on your device. See the privacy policy for the details.
It will not connect
Check the server and the port under Account in Settings. With Gmail, choosing "Sign in" is the reliable route (an ordinary password often will not connect).
It says a message could not be triaged
That happens when a message is very long, or the AI is busy. Leave it a moment and try again. You can also set the label yourself.
It says the AI is unavailable
Apple's built-in AI does not run on every device. Check under AI engine in Settings. Receiving, reading and sending mail all work even when the AI does not.
My iPhone and Mac will not connect to each other
Press "Start looking" on both, then type the six digits shown on the other screen. Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi and both apps are up to date.
I want to cancel my subscription
Cancel any time from Account, then Subscriptions, in the App Store. After cancelling you can still read the mail you have and use the address book.
